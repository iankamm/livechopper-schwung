## Playback effects

- **FM approximates Move's:** Amount and Freq wobble the playback as on
  Move, and the wobble fades out over the Decay you set, so short Decays
  touch only the start of the sound. Checked against Move's own output,
  on the device and by ear.

## Envelope

- **Decay and Gate's release approximate Move's:** longer Decays no
  longer drop away early.
