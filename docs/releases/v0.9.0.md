## Playback effects

- **FM is matched to Move:** Amount and Freq wobble the playback the
  way Move's FM does, and the wobble now fades out over the Decay you
  set, so short Decays touch only the start of the sound. It passes a
  held-out test against Move's own output, checked on the device and
  by ear.

## Envelope

- **Decay and Gate's release fade as Move's do:** smoothly in level
  rather than in amplitude, so longer Decays no longer drop away early.
