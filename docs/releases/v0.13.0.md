## 8-Bit

- **8-Bit approximates Move's:** quiet sounds keep much more detail than
  before, and the rate reduction follows Transpose and Detune.
- **8-Bit's Decay now darkens the sound** over the Decay time, as on
  Move. With Decay Off, the sound keeps Move's gentle top-end roll-off.
- **The Rate plays exactly as dialled**, not rounded to a whole Hz.
- Checked against Move's own output, on the device and by ear.
