## 8-Bit

- **8-Bit now sounds like Move's:** the bit reduction is Move's 8-bit
  A-law, which keeps quiet sounds far smoother than the old flat 8 bits,
  and the rate reduction holds the sound the way Move does, following
  Transpose and Detune.
- **8-Bit's Decay closes Move's low-pass:** from 20 kHz down towards
  300 Hz over the Decay time, the same curve as on Move. With Decay
  Off, the sound keeps Move's gentle top-end roll-off.
- **The Rate plays exactly as dialled**, not rounded to a whole Hz.
- Passes held-out tests against Move's own output, checked on the
  device and by ear.
