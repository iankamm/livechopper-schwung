## Move's volume envelope

- **Chop mode's volume envelope is now Move's:** a slice's level follows
  Attack, Hold and Gate as the exported pad's does on Move, so a chop
  sounds the same before and after export at any setting:
  - Attack: as on Move, its length sets where the level settles
    (a little louder at 1 ms than at the minimum, for example), and so
    does Transpose.
  - Hold: a finite Hold rises slightly to full level by its end, then the
    Decay begins.
  - Gate: the slice reaches full level right after the Attack.
- **Slices fade out at their end** over Move's 10 ms instead of stopping
  dead. Transposed, the fade follows Move's: a slice played higher stops
  partway through it, one played lower fades out a little before its end.
- **Attack starts at its minimum, 0.1 ms,** as the exported pads do,
  instead of at 0 until the knob was turned.
- Checked on the device against Move's own output.
