## Volume envelope

- **Chop mode's volume envelope approximates Move's:** Attack, Hold, Gate
  and Transpose now shape a slice's level as they do on the exported
  pad, so a chop sounds the same before and after export.
- **Slices fade out at their end** instead of stopping dead.
- **Attack starts at its minimum,** as the exported pads do, instead of
  at 0 until the knob was turned.
- Checked against Move's own output on the device.
