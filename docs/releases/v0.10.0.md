## Playback effects

- **Ring Mod is matched to Move:** it now shapes the sound the way
  Move's Ring Mod does: Amount sets the depth, Freq the rate (the level
  dips twice a cycle), and the modulation fades out over the Decay you
  set, leaving the sound a little louder than dry. It passes a held-out
  test against Move's own output, checked on the device and by ear.
