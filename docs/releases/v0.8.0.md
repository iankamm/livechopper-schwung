## Playback effects

- **Pitch Env is matched to Move:** 100 % is a 48-semitone bend that
  falls away exactly as Move's does, over the Decay you set. It passes a
  held-out test against Move's own output, checked on the device and by
  ear.

## Knobs

- **Effect, Filter Type and Envelope Mode turn more slowly:** one item
  every 4 detents, as in Schwung's own menus (hold Shift for one a
  detent). The nine effects now take about a sixth of a turn, not a few
  degrees.
- **Gain** reads out without a "+", like every other value.
