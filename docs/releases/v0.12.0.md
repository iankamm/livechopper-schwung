## Transpose and Detune

- **The envelope follows the pitch, as on Move:** Attack, Hold and
  Decay now cover the same part of the sample at any Transpose or
  Detune. Gate's release keeps its time.
- **Effects follow the pitch:** FM, Ring Mod and Sub Osc's Freq and
  Noise's Color move with Transpose and Detune, as on Move; the
  effects' own decays keep their time.
- Checked against Move's own output across a wide range of Transpose,
  on the device and by ear.
