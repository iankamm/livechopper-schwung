## Stretch

- **Stretch now plays like Move's:** the slice plays back in short grains,
  back to back, each with a 1 ms fade in and out, from a playhead that
  moves at 1/Factor. The pitch holds while the slice lasts Factor times as
  long. Grain Size sets each grain's length, and under Transpose the
  grains and their fades follow the pitch, as on Move.
- **A stretched slice fades out at its end** (Move's 10 ms fade) instead
  of clicking, and it stops when the playhead reaches the end.
- Stretch at Factor 1.00 is plain playback.
- Passes held-out tests against Move's own output, checked on the
  device and by ear.
