## Playback effects

- **Sub Osc is matched to Move:** each hit rings a low tone at Freq
  under the sound, at the level Amount sets, dying away over the Decay
  you set. As on Move, it sounds on past the end of a short slice.
- **Noise is matched to Move:** noise centred on Color follows the
  sound's own level and fades over the Decay. Amount now rises the way
  Move's does, so low settings are as quiet as on Move.
- Both pass held-out tests against Move's own output, checked on the
  device and by ear.
