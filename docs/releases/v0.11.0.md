## Playback effects

- **Sub Osc approximates Move's:** each hit rings a low tone at Freq
  under the sound, at the level Amount sets, dying away over the Decay
  you set. As on Move, it sounds on past the end of a short slice.
- **Noise approximates Move's:** noise centred on Color follows the
  sound's own level and fades over the Decay, and low Amounts are as
  quiet as on Move.
- Both checked against Move's own output, on the device and by ear.
