## Playback effects

- **Effect knob (Main 6):** Move's nine Drum Sampler playback effects, in
  Move's order: Stretch, Loop, Pitch Env, Punch, 8-Bit, FM, Ring Mod,
  Sub Osc, Noise. No Off, as on Move; the default (Stretch 1.00) plays and
  exports exactly as before.
- **Main 7 and 8** are the selected effect's two settings, with Move's
  ranges, curves and defaults. Each effect keeps its own values while you
  switch. 8-Bit gains its Decay on Knob 8.
- **Export** writes the effect and its settings into every pad of the
  drum rack preset.
- **Chop mode** plays a stand-in for each effect so you can hear roughly
  what you'll get. **Punch** is matched to Move: its gain curve was
  measured on the device, and it passes a held-out test against Move's
  own output.

## Knobs follow Move

- Attack, Hold, Decay, every effect setting, Filter Frequency, Resonance
  and Peak Gain now step, fine-step (Shift) and read out as Move's do:
  131 clicks end to end, Move's curves and ranges (Attack 0.10 ms-20 s,
  Hold to Inf, Decay to 60 s), and Move's formats ("0.10 ms", "1.03 s",
  no "+").
- Holding Shift turns Knob 5 into Length, as on Move (replacing
  Shift+tap). Decay and Length no longer have Shift fine steps.
- Hold's top value is now "Inf" (was "Full").
- Help text updated.
